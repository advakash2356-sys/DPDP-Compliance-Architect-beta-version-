    Breach clock
    
  
    Rule 7 requires you to tell affected people and the Board without delay. The detailed report to the Board is due within 72 hours of becoming aware, unless the Board allows longer on a written request. Rule 7 comes into force on 13 May 2027.
    
  
    
  
    What the notice to affected people must cover (Rule 7(1))
    1. A description of the breach: its nature, extent and when it happened.
    2. The likely consequences for them.
    3. The mitigation measures taken or under way.
    4. Safety measures they can take.
    5. Business contact details of a person who can answer their questions.

    Penalties (Schedule, s. 33)
    These are maximum amounts. The Board decides the actual penalty after an inquiry, considering the factors in s. 33(2).
