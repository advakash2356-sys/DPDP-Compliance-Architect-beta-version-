What the DPDP Act needs from you

If your business collects personal data in digital form, such as names, phone numbers or order details, these are the main duties. Most apply from 13 May 2027.

s. 5, Rule 3

Tell people

A clear notice of what you collect and why.

s. 6

Ask properly

Consent by a clear action, easy to withdraw.

s. 8(5), Rule 6

Keep it safe

Reasonable security, and logs kept for a year.

s. 8(6), Rule 7

Report breaches

Tell people and the Board, with a report within 72 hours.

s. 8(7), Rule 8

Delete on time

Erase data once its purpose is served.

s. 11–14

Answer requests

Access, correction, erasure, grievances and nomination.

Breach clock
Rule 7: tell affected people and the Board without delay. The detailed report to the Board is due within 72 hours of becoming aware, unless the Board allows longer on a written request.

   

   

   
Separate from DPDP: CERT-In's 2022 directions ask many organisations to report certain cyber incidents within 6 hours.
