Refund policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

The free trial costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund for it.

Paid subscriptions

The Practitioner plan is a digital service that you can use from the moment you pay. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and there are no further charges. We do not give partial refunds for unused days in a period that has already started, except in the cases below.

When we refund in full

  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • You were charged after you validly cancelled or withdrew your mandate.
  • You were charged without a valid mandate, or for a different amount than the pre-debit notice stated.
  • The service was unavailable for most of a paid period because of a fault on our side.

To ask for a refund, email grievance@example.invalid with your sign-in email and the payment date. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days of approval. Your bank may take longer to show the credit.

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

For your assistance only. Not legal advice. No assurance of compliance.
