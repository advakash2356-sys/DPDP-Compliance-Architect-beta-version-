Why it matters

The rules are written. Most small teams have not mapped them yet.

Rule 1 · DPDP Rules, 2025

Main duties apply from 13 May 2027

Notice, consent, security, breach reporting, erasure and rights all start on that date.

Schedule · s. 33

Penalties are set by the Board

Maximum amounts range from ₹10,000 to ₹250 crore. The Board decides the actual amount after an inquiry.

s. 8(1)

The duty stays with you

The business that decides why data is used stays responsible, even when a vendor does the processing.
