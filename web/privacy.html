Privacy notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

This notice is given under section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rule 3 of the DPDP Rules, 2025. The Data Fiduciary is the operator of DPDP Compliance Architect (operator details will be published here before launch).

What we collect, and why

  • When you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address, name and profile photo link. We use them to identify you and give you access.
  • Trial and subscription records: when your trial started, your plan status, your paid-until date and your Razorpay subscription ID. We use them to run your trial and subscription and to stop the trial being reset.
  • If you subscribe: Razorpay processes your payment details and shares only the payment status and IDs with us.
  • Tool inputs (your self-assessment answers, the details you type into a notice, and breach times): these are worked out entirely in your browser and are not sent to our server. They are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) until you reset them or clear your browser data. The public self-check page is blocked from making any network request.
  • Visit counts: the home page sends an anonymous visit event (a random ID stored in your browser, the page path and any ?src= tag) so we can count visits. It contains no name, email or tool input. Tool pages send no such events.
  • Owner dashboard: the site owner signs in with Google to view aggregate counts of sign-ups and visits. Only listed owner accounts can open it.
  • Hosting logs: our host (Vercel) records technical request data, such as IP address, browser type and time, for security and operation.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data. Your sign-in token is held in your browser's session storage and is deleted when you close the tab or sign out.

Who processes it for us

Google (sign-in, and the Google Sheet that stores account records), Vercel (hosting) and Razorpay (payments, once subscriptions open). Some of these may process data outside India, as section 16 of the Act permits.

How long we keep it

We keep your account record while your account is active. If you ask us to erase it, we delete it, except for payment records that tax and accounting law requires us to keep.

Your rights

You may withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by emailing us. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it. You may also ask for a summary of your data, or for correction, completion, updating or erasure, and you may nominate someone to act for you (sections 11 to 14). Write to grievance@example.invalid. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This service is meant for adults. Please do not sign in if you are under 18.
