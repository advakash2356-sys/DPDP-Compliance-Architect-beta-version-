Privacy notice

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

This notice is given under section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rule 3 of the DPDP Rules, 2025. The Data Fiduciary is Adv. Akash Sharma, the operator of DPDP Compliance Architect.

What we collect, and why

  • When you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address, name and profile photo link. We use them to identify you and give you access.
  • Trial and subscription records: when your trial started, your plan status, your paid-until date and your Razorpay subscription ID. We use them to run your trial and subscription and to stop the trial being reset.
  • If you subscribe: Razorpay processes your payment details and shares only the payment status and IDs with us.
  • Tool inputs (your self-assessment answers and the details you type into a notice): these are sent to our server only so it can work out the result. They are not stored on the server. Copies are kept only in your own browser (localStorage) until you reset them or clear your browser data.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data. Your sign-in token is held in your browser's session storage and is deleted when you close the tab or sign out.

Who processes it for us

Google (sign-in, and the Google Sheet that stores account records), Vercel (hosting) and Razorpay (payments, once subscriptions open). Some of these may process data outside India, as section 16 of the Act permits.

How long we keep it

We keep your account record while your account is active. If you ask us to erase it, we delete it, except for payment records that tax and accounting law requires us to keep.

Your rights

You may withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by emailing us. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it. You may also ask for a summary of your data, or for correction, completion, updating or erasure, and you may nominate someone to act for you (sections 11 to 14). Write to adv.akash2356@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This service is meant for adults. Please do not sign in if you are under 18.
