Pricing and subscription terms

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Free during soft launch

All tools are free while we test them. No payment is taken on this site and no paid plan is open. If that changes, the price, including GST, will be published here first, and nothing will be charged without your clear agreement.

Workspace sign-in

The workspace needs a Google sign-in. Your access record (including a trial date used by the system) is kept as described in the Privacy notice.

If paid plans open later: automatic renewal

If you choose to subscribe, the plan renews automatically every month at the then-current price until you cancel. Renewals are collected by Razorpay through a card, UPI Autopay or bank e-mandate that you approve. Under the Reserve Bank of India's framework for e-mandates on recurring transactions:

  • you authenticate the mandate when you set it up (additional factor of authentication);
  • you receive a pre-debit notification at least 24 hours before each renewal charge, with the amount and date, and you can opt out of that charge;
  • you can view, modify or withdraw the mandate at any time, through your card issuer, your bank or your UPI app;
  • renewals above the RBI limit for frictionless recurring debits (currently ₹15,000 for most categories) need fresh authentication each time.

If we ever change the price, we will tell you by email before the change applies to your next renewal.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. We never see or store your full card or bank details.
